How far do you travel?

I'm based in Dallas and regularly travel across Texas and beyond. Travel fees are quoted based on location.

How soon should we book?

For weddings, 6–12 months is a comfortable planning window. Portrait and brand sessions can often be scheduled sooner.

How do we reserve our date?

After your consultation, you'll receive the proposal and agreement. Your date is officially reserved once the signed agreement and retainer are received.

Can we customize a package?

Absolutely. The packages above are starting points; we'll build the right coverage around your plans.
